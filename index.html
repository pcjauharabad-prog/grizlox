Project Overview

GRIZLOX (GRZL) is a decentralized Web3 ecosystem built on Binance Smart Chain (BEP-20). It operates through smart contracts ensuring transparency, security, and decentralization. The ecosystem is fully community-driven and focuses on long-term liquidity-based growth and Web3 expansion.
